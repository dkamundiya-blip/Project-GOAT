## 3. Knowledge Graph Design

The `ResearchKnowledgeGraph` models relationships between research entities without probabilistic assumptions:

### Entity Node Types (`NodeType`)
- `FEATURE`: Quantitative engineered market features.
- `HYPOTHESIS`: Research hypotheses (`HYP_<HEX16>`).
- `EDGE`: Statistically validated edges (`EDG_<HEX16>`).
- `REGIME`: 5-D market regime classifications (`BULL_TREND`, `HIGH_VOLATILITY`, etc.).
- `SYMBOL`: Derivative synthetic index symbols (`BOOM_1000`, `CRASH_500`, etc.).
- `SESSION`: Market session resolutions.
- `TIMEFRAME`: Bar resolutions (`1m`, `5m`, `1h`, etc.).
- `VALIDATION_RESULT`: Empirical walk-forward and statistical test records.

### Relationship Edge Types (`EdgeType`)
- `DERIVED_FROM`: Edge derived from quantitative feature combination.
- `EVALUATED_BY`: Edge evaluated by candidate hypothesis.
- `VALIDATED_ON`: Edge validated on historical observation dataset.
- `ACTIVE_IN`: Edge demonstrated active in specific market regime.
- `APPLIES_TO`: Edge applicable to target symbol and timeframe.
- `DECAYS_IN`: Edge exhibiting performance decay in specific market conditions.

## 4. Reasoning Pipeline & Evidence Model

Every deduction generated by the `ReasoningEngine` follows a strict deterministic pipeline:

```
[DiscoveredEdge] ──► [EvidenceEngine] ──► [EvidenceBundle] ──► [ReasoningEngine] ──► [ReasoningConclusion]
```

### Evidence Model (`EvidenceRecord` & `EvidenceBundle`)
Every conclusion statement is linked to specific `EvidenceRecord` items containing:
- `claim`: Natural language claim statement.
- `metric_name`: Name of empirical metric (`expected_value`, `sharpe_ratio`, `p_value`, `sample_size`, `oos_expected_value`).
- `metric_value`: Empirical numerical value from research outputs.
- `threshold_value`: Institutional hurdle rate or validation threshold.
- `is_supporting`: Boolean indicating whether evidence supports or refutes the claim.
- `canonical_hash`: SHA-256 canonical hash digest (`EVR_<HEX16>`, `EVB_<HEX16>`).

## 5. Natural Language Persona Translation Layer

Translates quantitative research conclusions across 3 target audience personas:

1. **BEGINNER**:
   - *Example*: "This trading pattern (trend_strength) has shown a consistent winning record of 60% over 100 past market tests on BOOM_1000. On average, it generates a small positive gain per test with a low risk of big losses."
2. **INTERMEDIATE**:
   - *Example*: "Edge EDG_0000000000000001 on BOOM_1000 (1m) has an Expected Return of 0.5000% per trade with a Win Rate of 60.0%. The Sharpe Ratio is 2.50 and the Profit Factor is 2.00 across 100 samples."
3. **PROFESSIONAL_QUANT**:
   - *Example*: "DiscoveredEdge EDG_0000000000000001 [Features: trend_strength] demonstrates statistically significant positive expectancy (EV = 0.005000, Sharpe = 2.5000, Sortino = 3.0000, Calmar = 4.0000) over N = 100 observations. Null hypothesis rejected at p-value = 0.010000 (Cohen's d = 0.8000, 95% CI [0.002000, 0.008000]). Composite Score = 0.8500."
